# Brand & authority reporting contacts for phishing/smishing

Verified August 2026 from each organization's own site. These addresses do
occasionally change — if one bounces or seems off, WebSearch
`"<brand>" report phishing email official` to find a current one rather than
guessing.

## Delivery / shipping carriers

- **USPS** — spam@uspis.gov. Include the message text (don't click the
  link), your name, and a screenshot showing the sender's phone number and
  the date/time received. Also forward the original text to 7726.
  Source: uspis.gov (Smishing: Package Tracking Text Scams)

- **FedEx** — abuse@fedex.com. Forward the phishing email or a description
  of the text directly. Source: fedex.com/en-us/report-fraud.html

- **UPS** — no dedicated abuse inbox published. Use the "Email UPS" contact
  form linked from UPS's fraud page:
  https://www.ups.com/us/en/support/shipping-support/legal-terms-conditions/fight-fraud
  If a form isn't practical to fill from this workflow, fall back to the
  universal contacts below and mention the form link to the user.

- **DHL** — phishing@dhl.com. For SMS, include a screenshot and the
  sender's phone number. Source: dhl.com (Fraud Awareness)

## Retail / tech

- **Amazon** — reportascam@amazon.com **and** stop-spoofing@amazon.com.
  Send the message as an attachment where possible, to preserve headers.

- **Apple** — reportphishing@apple.com. For texts, attach a screenshot of
  the message. Source: support.apple.com/en-us/102568

## Government / tax

- **IRS** — phishing@irs.gov. Subject line "IRS" or "Treasury" for
  phishing emails; subject line "Text" for smishing, including the
  sender's phone number, message contents, recipient's phone number, and
  date/time/time zone received. Also forward the text to 7726.
  Source: irs.gov (Report fake IRS, Treasury or tax-related emails and
  messages)

## Banks and financial institutions

There's no universal address — go directly to the real bank's official
fraud/security contact, found on their real website or the back of the
customer's card, never a number or link from the suspicious message
itself. Also file with APWG and the FTC below.

## Universal fallbacks

Use these when the impersonated brand isn't listed above, or in addition
to a brand-specific report:

- **APWG** — reportphishing@apwg.org for email (use "Forward As
  Attachment" if your email client supports it); https://apwg.org/sms for
  SMS/smishing submissions. Accepts reports for any impersonated
  organization.

- **FTC** — https://reportfraud.ftc.gov (web form, not email).

- **FBI IC3** — https://www.ic3.gov/complaint (web form; best when money
  or personal information was actually lost).

## SMS carrier-level reporting (US)

Forwarding the original text to **7726 ("SPAM")** reports the sending
number to the recipient's carrier for blocking. This works across all
major US carriers and is separate from, and in addition to, the email
reports above. It has no equivalent for non-US sending numbers — there,
rely on the registrar, brand, and APWG reports instead.
